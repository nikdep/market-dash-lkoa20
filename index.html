ⓘ How this works

Stocks with the biggest upside if a narrative from your posts plays out. "Since the call" = first close after the post to the latest close. Research only, not advice; the stop is where the story looks wrong, not a trade instruction. Tap a row to open the full card.

How ranked (small caps first, Oct 2): 1) filters: late calls (20%+ run in the prior month), price broken since the call, or under $2M/day traded go to the watchlist. 2) confirmation: Confirmed once the price moves with the story after the call (a day up 5%+ on 1.5× average volume, or a close above its 20-day high). 3) Confirmed names are ranked by upside ÷ risk × size weight (under $2B ×2.0, $2-10B ×1.4, $10-200B ×0.85); mega caps over $200B move to the Large caps strip at the bottom. Upside estimate = half the way back to the 52-week high + half the analyst mean target (capped +50%; ignored if stale or under $1B with < 4 analysts); risk = distance to a story-wrong stop (the tighter of 1% below the pre-call low and 2× the 14-day ATR). Waiting names: combined score × the same size weight. Low-float names are flagged. Backtest (top 5 per week, 1-month excess vs S&P, pop/bust ±20%): small-cap version -1.8% avg, 44% hit, pop 8%, bust 10% (n=267); previous combined -0.2% avg, 48% hit, pop 9%, bust 8% (n=337) (difference -3.8 to +0.6 pts, mostly from moving mega caps out). Confirmation does fix small caps: confirmed small caps -0.3% avg, 45% hit, pop 15%, bust 16% (n=73) vs all small-cap calls -3.9% avg, 34% hit, pop 13%, bust 28% (n=238). Earlier ranks (v4 / upside / echo / old) are on each card and in api/candidates.json.

Glossary
Confirmed
The price made the scan's qualifying move after the call.
Waiting
The idea is still being watched; that qualifying move has not happened.
Stop
A story-wrong level where the idea would look invalid; it is not an instruction to sell.
Risk %
How far the latest price is above that stop.
Upside est.
A rough room-to-run estimate using the 52-week high and analyst data where allowed.
Up/risk ratio
Estimated upside divided by risk to the stop; higher is better for this ranking.
Short % of float
The share of tradable shares sold short, which can add fuel but also signals skepticism.
Days to cover
Approximate trading days for short sellers to buy back their shares.
Low float
Few tradable shares, so the price can swing sharply both ways.
Source grade A–D
A quick read of evidence quality and independence; it only nudges the rank.
Copycats / echo
Later posts repeating an earlier idea; many repeats are not many independent discoveries.
Vs S&P
The stock return minus the S&P 500 return over the same period.
